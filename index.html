Furat Beverages & Products

Whater was born from a simple belief: everyone deserves access to water that's genuinely clean. Not just "treated" — but meticulously filtered, rigorously tested, and sealed with the care it deserves.

Manufactured at our facility in Plot No. 3A, Gausiya Society, Ring Road, Nagpur, every bottle passes through a multi-stage purification process before it reaches your hands.

We don't believe in shortcuts. We don't add artificial minerals, flavours, or preservatives. We believe the best water is water at its most essential — clean, balanced, and alive.

How We Do It

From Source
to Sealed.

01
Source Water

Water is drawn from a verified, licensed municipal or bore source and logged for traceability.

02
Pre-Filtration

Sediment and carbon pre-filters remove particulates, chlorine, and organic matter.

03
RO Purification

Reverse osmosis membranes eliminate dissolved solids, heavy metals, and microorganisms at the molecular level.

04
UV Sterilisation

UV-C light neutralises any residual biological contaminants for complete microbial safety.

05
Seal & Ship

Bottles are filled, tamper-sealed, batch-coded, and dispatched — ready to deliver pure water to you.
