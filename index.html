How We Do It

The journey to
absolute purity

01
Water Source

Sourced from protected deep aquifers and municipal supply, selected for optimal mineral balance.

02
RO Filtration

Reverse osmosis at 0.0001 micron precision eliminates dissolved solids, heavy metals, and contaminants.

03
UV Treatment

Ultraviolet sterilization neutralizes 99.99% of bacteria, viruses, and pathogens without chemicals.

04
Ozonization

Ozone injection ensures complete disinfection while preserving the clean, neutral taste profile.

05
Quality Testing

Every batch tested in our in-house laboratory against 30+ parameters before approval.

06
Sealed & Packed

Filled and capped in a sterile environment. Tamper-evident seal guarantees untouched purity.
